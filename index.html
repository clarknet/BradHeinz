A simple model that sticks

Two octaves. Two roads. A practical way to stop being pushed around by your internal state.

Reactive vs Proactive

  • Reactive: conditions dictate how you feel; you’re at the mercy of triggers.
  • Proactive: you choose your response; you gain freedom regardless of conditions.

The more proactive you become with your emotions, the more you experience freedom—not as a concept, but as something you can actually do.

“Low road is ground level—limited view. High road gives elevation—vistas and the big picture. The higher you go, the wider you can see.”

One of the core metaphors in the course (experienced, not just understood).

Power vs force

Lower octaves feel heavy and compelling—like gravity. Higher octaves carry more real power: clearer perception, more choice, less reactivity.

Ocean-to-air metaphor

Lower states can feel like deep water—pressure, darkness, limited options. As you rise, you get oxygen and light. Break the surface and you see the sunlight directly.

Example “hook”: Pride (a tricky note)

Pride is complex because it’s constantly valuing and judging. Once you can spot it as it happens, you stop being owned by it—and your relationships get cleaner fast.
